## 3. Steal / Adapt / Avoid

### Steal

- Light paper plane instead of black den.
- Illustration as the person, not a corner garnish.
- Soft ink ladder: name dark, body quieter, meta faintest.
- One dusty accent (coral), not a rainbow pastel kit.

### Adapt

- Her paper is cool/blue. Fox fur is warm — shift field to dusty linen/sand so the fox sits in the page.
- Gilroy → keep Fraunces + Source Serif + IBM mono (existing fox stack).
- Coral `#BF6F4E` lifted toward pastel clay (`#C4846A` class) so it reads grounded, not lipstick.

### Avoid

- Copying the penguin, fishing game, or her nav.
- Ice-blue pastel field (fights the fox).
- Candy pink / mint fills.
- Terracotta button spam — accent stays text/hover only.
