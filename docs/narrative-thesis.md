# Narrative Thesis

## One-line thesis

I design and write to turn uncertainty into form, using minimal geometry to document a living process of becoming.

## Three-part site arc

1. Origin: Where my questions began and why visual reduction matters to me.
2. Experiments: Projects and writing that show attempts, failures, revisions, and decisions.
3. Becoming: What I am learning now, what I still do not know, and where I am heading.

## Tone rules

- Vulnerable and precise.
- Concrete over abstract.
- Evidence over declarations.
- Quiet confidence, no self-mythology.

## Anti-pretension filter

Before publishing any page or post, remove lines that:

- claim impact without showing process;
- use poetic language with no concrete artifact;
- sound like branding copy instead of lived reflection.

## Core copy blocks

Use these as defaults across site sections.

### Hero statement

Minimal forms, honest notes, ongoing work.

### Hero subtext

This space tracks my design process and personal growth, one iteration at a time.

### About opener

I am not building a perfect image of myself here. I am collecting proof of how I think, make, fail, and learn.

### Journal opener

Each entry starts with a question and ends with a next step.
