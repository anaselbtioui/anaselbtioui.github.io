# Harbor CRM

## Snapshot

- Date: 2025-11
- Role: Product + engineering
- Duration: 14 weeks
- Domain: CRM · AI

## Problem

A trades co-op with 40 field staff kept clients in WhatsApp threads and a shared spreadsheet. Quotes drifted, follow-ups vanished, and nobody trusted the pipeline number on Monday mornings.

## Artistic Intent

Calm ops software — fewer screens, clearer next action. Feels like a desk cleared for one job, not a SaaS carnival.

## Process

### 1) Question

What is the smallest system that makes “who do I call today?” obvious without training?

### 2) Attempt

Shipped a contact + deal board with AI note summaries from pasted call transcripts. Mobile-first for vans.

### 3) Failure

First AI summaries hallucinated next steps. Reps stopped trusting the panel within a week.

### 4) Revision

Constrained the model to extract only: people, dates, amounts, open questions. Human confirms before anything writes to the deal.

### 5) Outcome

Quote cycle time dropped from ~9 days to ~4. Pipeline board used daily by 31 of 40 staff after six weeks.

## Reflection

- What this project taught me about my craft: AI is useful when it is narrow and checkable.
- What still feels unresolved: Offline mode for dead-signal sites.
- Next experiment I will run: Voice capture that never invents a follow-up.
