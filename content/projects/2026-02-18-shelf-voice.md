# Shelf Voice

## Snapshot

- Date: 2026-02
- Role: Design + build
- Duration: 9 weeks
- Domain: Inventory · Messaging

## Problem

A small retailer tracked stock on paper pads behind the counter. Night counts never matched; reorder guesses caused empty shelves on weekends.

## Artistic Intent

Stock updates that feel like texting a colleague — zero training UI, zero “system.”

## Process

### 1) Question

Can staff update counts without leaving WhatsApp?

### 2) Attempt

WhatsApp bot: photo of shelf → AI count estimate → confirm number → write to inventory sheet.

### 3) Failure

Lighting and stacked boxes wrecked counts. Staff quit after three bad evenings.

### 4) Revision

Dropped photo counting. Structured messages only: `SKU 14` or `flour -2`. AI only parses intent; humans own the number.

### 5) Outcome

Daily counts landed for 5 locations. Weekend stockouts on top 20 SKUs fell noticeably in the first month.

## Reflection

- What this project taught me about my craft: Meet people in the channel they already live in.
- What still feels unresolved: Multi-language slang at the till.
- Next experiment I will run: Soft reminders when a SKU goes quiet for N days.
