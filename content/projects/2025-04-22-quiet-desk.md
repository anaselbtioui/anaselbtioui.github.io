# Quiet Desk

## Snapshot

- Date: 2025-04
- Role: Solo build
- Duration: 3 weeks
- Domain: Internal tools

## Problem

My own freelance ops lived in four tabs: mail, Notion, Stripe, calendar. Context switching ate mornings before any client work started.

## Artistic Intent

One morning surface — today only. No infinite backlog theatre.

## Process

### 1) Question

What do I actually need before 10am?

### 2) Attempt

A single page: today’s calls, unpaid invoices, and one “ship this” task pulled from Notion + Stripe APIs.

### 3) Failure

First version showed everything. It became another anxious dashboard.

### 4) Revision

Hard cap: three items max per lane. Overflow goes to “later” with no badge counts.

### 5) Outcome

Morning startup ritual dropped from ~40 minutes of tab archaeology to under ten.

## Reflection

- What this project taught me about my craft: Constraints are the product.
- What still feels unresolved: Mobile glance view.
- Next experiment I will run: End-of-day “clear the desk” archive gesture.
