# Origin: Why Reduction, Not Minimalism

Date: 2026-05-01
Tags: self-discovery-log, design-philosophy

## Question
Why do I keep removing things until only structure remains? What am I trying to protect?

## Attempt
I rewrote my “About” page three times with one constraint: no adjectives unless backed by an example. I also sketched 20 variations of circle + line, trying to express “continuity + direction” without icons or illustrations.

## Failure
Version 1 sounded like branding. Version 2 sounded like apology. Version 3 was honest but flat: it had facts, no pulse.

## Insight
Reduction is not “less style.” It is a way to stop performing and start proving. If I cannot show it in process artifacts, I should not say it.

## Artifact
- draft: `about-v1.md`, `about-v2.md`, `about-v3.md` (placeholders)
- sketch: 20 circle/line studies (placeholder scan)

## Humility Line
What I still do not know: how to be direct without becoming cold.

## Next Step
Write one paragraph that includes one concrete failure and one concrete revision, then stop.
