# Next Step: Template Smell Test Ritual

Date: 2026-05-05
Tags: process-note, design

## Question
How do I keep originality when my brain keeps reaching for familiar layouts?

## Attempt
I made a monthly ritual: open three popular portfolios, then open mine. I give myself 3 seconds per page to answer one question: “Have I seen this silhouette before?” If yes, I must change structure, not colors.

## Failure
I tried to “fix it” with micro styling. It still read like a template because the structure was unchanged: hero, cards, testimonial vibe, footer. Polishing a familiar skeleton stays familiar.

## Insight
Originality is mostly architecture. Visual craft matters, but structure is the first tell. I need constraints that force different layout decisions, not different gradients.
