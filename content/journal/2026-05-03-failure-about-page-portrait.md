# Failure: Portrait-First About Page Felt Wrong

Date: 2026-05-03
Tags: process-note, self-discovery-log

## Question
Why does a typical “Hi, I’m…” layout make me uncomfortable, even when it looks good?

## Attempt
I mocked a conventional About section: photo, short bio, skills grid. Then I rewrote it with a different constraint: “no identity claims; only decisions, constraints, and evidence.”

## Failure
The portrait-first version felt like asking for trust before offering proof. The skills grid reduced me to a checklist. It was clean, but it was not me.

## Insight
I want the About page to behave like a lab notebook: context first, then evidence, then invitation. My face can appear later as a footnote, not an entrance.

## Artifact
- screenshot: About v1 (portrait-first) vs v2 (evidence-first) (placeholders)
- draft: “evidence-first about” paragraph (placeholder)

## Humility Line
What I still do not know: how much personal context is enough before it becomes self-focus.

## Next Step
Add a single “Now” paragraph and two small artifacts, then stop adding sections.
