How to respectfully blend a sports arena into a historical setting

SAP Garden

SAP Garden echoes the same democratic ideals as its historic surroundings, uniting sports and local community in a state-of-the-art sports arena. It is the new home of ice hockey team EHC Red Bull Munich and basketball club FC Bayern Munich.

The bowl can gather up to 10,700 spectators for ice hockey and 11,500 for basketball. Beyond the sports facilities, the arena offers VIP areas, shops, and public space.

Information

  • Client: Red Bull Stadion München GmbH
  • Location: Olympic Park, Munich, Germany
  • Size: 62.500 m²
  • Collaborators: CL MAP, Latz + Partner
  • Status: Completed 2024
  • Program: Sports arena

“We are pleased to be opening the new SAP Garden arena, marking a new era for sport in the city of Munich. The project has been a meaningful opportunity for us to extend the architectural legacy of the Olympic Park.”

module-text · left

Melting into its historic context

Adding a new structure into a protected landscape posed a complex design challenge: how to respect the integrity of the park while accommodating the spatial demands of a modern arena.

The sports facilities are nestled in green terrain which rolls and undulates around them — human scale first, spectacle second.

A unique geometry

The façade is dynamic and welcoming with vertical pilaster strips that swing upwards and accentuate glass-panelled entrances. Each strip is unique in geometry.

module-text · right

Place for the public

Designed as a catalyst for activity — on game days and off days. Merchandise, e-sport community space, restaurant with bowls overlooking the park.
